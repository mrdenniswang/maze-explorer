# Maze Explorer

Mazes for young kids, made for playing on an iPad or computer.

**Play:** https://mrdenniswang.github.io/maze-explorer/

- Easy (11×11 garden), Medium (16×16 ocean) and Hard (22×22 space) levels, with a new maze every time
- Square, circle, heart and diamond maze shapes
- Treasures to collect for bonus stars
- A daily maze with a sticker calendar
- Stars unlock new characters to play as

Draw a line with your finger (or mouse) from your character to its goal, or use the arrow buttons/keys.
Progress is saved in the browser on each device.
